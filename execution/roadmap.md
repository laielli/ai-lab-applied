# Roadmap

Current priorities organized by timeframe.

## Now
*What we're actively working on*

- **Literature Survey**: Review recent text-to-video retrieval papers (CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS 2023-2025)
- **Identify Gaps**: Analyze where temporal reasoning and cross-modal alignment fall short
- **Dataset Familiarity**: Understand MSR-VTT, DiDeMo, ActivityNet Captions, LSMDC benchmarks

## Next
*What's coming up*

- **Baseline Implementation**: Reproduce CLIP4Clip, X-CLIP, or similar baselines
- **Evaluation Infrastructure**: Set up standard retrieval metrics (R@1, R@5, R@10, MdR, MnR)
- **Initial Paper Proposal**: Draft PRD for first research direction based on identified gaps

## Later
*Future ideas and explorations*

- **Temporal Reasoning Methods**: Develop novel approaches for capturing temporal structure
- **Cross-Modal Alignment Improvements**: Explore fine-grained text-video alignment
- **Efficiency Optimizations**: Investigate sparse attention and hierarchical representations
- **Multi-Benchmark Evaluation**: Comprehensive testing across all major datasets
