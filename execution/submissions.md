# Submissions

Conference and journal submission calendar.

## Target Venues

### Vision + Language (Primary)
- **CVPR** — June deadline, main venue for video understanding
- **ICCV** — March deadline (odd years), top vision venue
- **ECCV** — March deadline (even years), top vision venue

### Machine Learning
- **NeurIPS** — May deadline, broad ML audience
- **ICML** — January deadline, methods-focused

### NLP + Multimodal
- **ACL** — February deadline, language-focused with multimodal track
- **EMNLP** — June deadline, strong multimodal presence
- **NAACL** — January deadline (some years)
